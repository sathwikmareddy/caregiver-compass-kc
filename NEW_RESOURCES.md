# New features added October 30, 2026

This revision focuses on a small, usable November 8 demo rather than creating a production support marketplace.

## Resources added

- **Financial counseling and wellness:** KU Health System and University Health billing counselors, Triage Cancer financial guides, Patient Advocate Foundation one-on-one case management.
- **Treatment and insurance costs:** TotalAssist, HealthWell, CancerCare co-pay foundation, HNC Living Foundation diagnosis-specific aid.
- **Donated items:** Cancer Action wigs, scarves and comfort items alongside its existing medical equipment listing.
- **Volunteer/practical help:** MyLifeLine Helping Calendar and CaringBridge task requests, plus the existing volunteer ride and cleaning programs.
- **Receiving donations:** Personal fundraising resources with consent, privacy and fee caveats.
- **Giving back:** A new Donate & volunteer tab with seven reputable organization links. These are not payment forms.

## Three demonstration journeys

**Caregiver A — Financial wellness:** Type “My mother has medical bills and I need budgeting and financial counseling.” Choose Financial assistance and Insurance and medical bills. Explain financial education versus direct grants.

**Caregiver B — Supplies and helpers:** Type “I need donated medical equipment and volunteers to help with meals and rides.” Select Jackson County. Show that a donated item program has availability restrictions and that MyLifeLine coordinates trusted contacts, not a new volunteer dispatch service.

**Community member C — Give back:** Click Donate & volunteer; filter to Volunteer your time or Donate supplies. Open an official organization page; the app does not collect money or volunteer applications.

## Safety and accuracy

Open funds and local volunteer capacity can change daily. Each program may require documentation and has individual eligibility requirements. The prototype provides contacts, not referrals, bookings, money or confirmed services. Do not share patient-identifying details in public fundraisers without consent.

## Run

Extract the ZIP and open index.html. For automated checks, run `node --test tests/*.test.js` in the extracted folder.
