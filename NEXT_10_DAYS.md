## Scope freeze

**Must have:** working intake, reasoned recommendations, verified source links, top three steps, responsive UI, documentation and usable demo.

**Nice to have only if time remains:** multilingual translation, optional consent-based LLM language interpretation, expanded database. Do not attempt hospital EHR integration, live eligibility checking, login systems, paid cloud infrastructure, medical advice, or a full chatbot before Nov 8.

## What to tell the team

This is an explainable, local-first **caregiver resource matching** MVP, intentionally scoped to common needs and a curated set of official resources. The primary research question is whether a simple guided workflow helps overwhelmed caregivers identify useful next steps with fewer searches.


## Added feature: Cancer Terms Explained (October 30, 2026)

The prototype also contains a searchable 61-term, six-category cancer-care glossary. A caregiver can look up medical abbreviations, diagnostic words, side effects, caregiver services and health-insurance terminology. Each entry offers a simple definition, why it may come up, a useful question to ask the clinical team, and a link to an authoritative reference. A matching word in the free-text intake can link directly to its definition. This is educational content only, not medical interpretation. Demonstrate a search for “neutropenia” and “palliative care” to show the caregiver-centered approach.
