THE PROJECT

Technology that meets caregivers where they are.

Caregiver Compass KC is a proof of concept developed for a UMKC cancer caregiver support project.

◎

Our goal

Help someone go from “I don't know where to begin” to “I have a next step.” The website identifies needs, matches caregivers to actionable resources, and explains unfamiliar cancer-care terms in plain language.

◇

How recommendations work

An explainable text-and-category matching engine prioritizes resources relevant to the caregiver's concerns, location and journey stage. It does not generate medical advice or make eligibility decisions.

▣

Privacy by design

This prototype runs locally in your browser. It does not collect accounts, send entered text to an AI provider, or save health information to a server. Do not enter personal or private medical information.

✦

What comes next

A weekly source scanner can flag new links from approved providers for human review. Approved entries can then be added to the resource library after checking the service, contact details, restrictions, and last-reviewed date. No unreviewed results appear automatically.

Safety signposts: The intake can flag some clearly concerning phrases and redirect to 911 or an oncology team, but it cannot reliably recognize every emergency or provide clinical triage. A person should never wait for, or rely on, a website alert before seeking care.
Important: This is a student demonstration, not a medical service, clinical decision tool, referral or availability guarantee. For medical questions, contact your care team. For immediate emergencies call 911 (U.S.).

Medical emergency: Call 911 in the United States for life-threatening symptoms such as severe breathing difficulty or serious chest pain.
