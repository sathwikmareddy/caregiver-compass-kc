**Separation rule:** Asking to receive assistance does not imply willingness to donate. Organizational donation pages must never be represented as assistance applications or benefits approvals. The app does not process payments, accept donations, register volunteers, match strangers or guarantee funding.

## Accuracy rule

Never say “You qualify”, “A ride is available”, or “Funding is approved”. Say “Contact the provider to check requirements and current availability.”
